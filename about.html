About Our QR ID System

A faster, safer, and smarter way to identify students.

STUDENT ID

What is the QR ID System?

Our QR ID System gives every student a school ID with a unique QR code. By scanning the QR code, the school can quickly check who the student is and record their attendance, with no more long lines or paper logbooks.

How It Works

1

Get Your ID

Each student receives an ID with their own QR code.

2

Scan

The QR code is scanned at the school entrance or in class.

3

Recorded

The system saves the student's attendance automatically.

Why We Made It

Faster entry and attendance checking

Better security, so only students can enter

Less paper and fewer mistakes

Easy to keep track of student records
